Project Infinity X 4.1 (Android 17) for the Motorola ThinkPhone
Build 2026-10-18  UNOFFICIAL

Fixes in this build
-------------------
- The camera/microphone privacy dot no longer sits on top of the battery, and
  the status bar is symmetric: the battery's gap on the right mirrors the
  clock's gap on the left.

- Quick settings and the shade header line up with the status bar inset in
  every orientation and on every screen size; the value is derived from the
  status bar inset instead of being a second number kept in sync by hand.

- Keyguard carrier text is no longer inset twice.

Everything from the 2026-10-17 build is included: double-tap to wake,
flicker-free refresh rate, a single refresh-rate choice, AOD at 60 Hz, the
stock Motorola camera, and updates served from this project.

Known issues
------------
- USB-C port role selection is unreliable.

Report problems with the build date from Settings > About phone and a logcat.
Dirty flashing over the previous build works; factory reset before reporting
if anything misbehaves.
